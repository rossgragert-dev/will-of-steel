# v0.2.1 — The Art of the Blade

Challenge-only rules: seeded enemy feints with a readable second windup, armor-zone damage, deterministic early attack interruption, small advance/retreat and torso motion, and reduced direction prompts. Training retains original combat balance and direction cues. Both modes now measure closest points between rendered sword blade segments, including six interpolated sweep steps per frame; clash/parry effects use the measured contact when sufficiently close in space/time, otherwise retain zoned feedback. This does NOT make collisions authoritative for damage or parries. Five-zone taps and hold/toggle controls unchanged. Previous baseline archived. 51 logic/geometry checks in isolated JavaScript harness; actual Three.js rendering, sword contact frequency, touch feel, and challenge balance remain unverified on physical device.
