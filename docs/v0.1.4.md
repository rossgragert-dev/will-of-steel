# v0.1.4 — Guard visibility and weapon handling

Based on the iPhone recording of v0.1.3, the near-camera rigid forearm inverted into view during the lower-right parry. Both low guards now hold the hilt lower and farther from the camera, with outward/downward blades. The player and opponent have independent tapered arm segments with wrists tracking two points on the sword grip. The player arms no longer rotate with the weapon. Neutral pose shifts the blade right of the opponent. Adds lightweight zoned hit/clash feedback, opponent hit recoil, smoother frame-rate-adjusted weapon poses, and small sword grip detail. Keeps five-zone taps, hold/toggle and combat rules unchanged. Geometry/logic tests pass in JS harness; actual WebGL, depth/visibility, animation quality and physical iPhone validation remain outstanding. This is procedural arm tracking, not full skeletal animation or physical collision.
