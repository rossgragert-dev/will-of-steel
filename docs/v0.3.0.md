# v0.3.0 — The Living Blade (first animation pass)

First hand-authored animation library: five seven-key attacks for each of the enemy and first-person player (ten clips), mirrored side variants, existing five first-person parry poses preserved. Blade direction and edge alignment sampled along windup/chamber/contact/follow-through/recovery paths; clip contact normalized to the existing combat impact time. Rigid plate armor is attached to a new hips/chest/thigh/shin/foot Bone hierarchy; analytic leg IK and pole-biased arm joint solving, crouch and torso rotation, procedural stepping and clash recoil. This is not motion capture, a deforming skinned humanoid or the complete proposed 36-clip library. Includes optional half-speed training preview. Combat remains timing-based; physical blade contact still drives effects only. No new asset dependencies. Real Three.js rendering, joint limits, clipping, foot planting, visual authenticity and device performance must be checked on iPhone. Previous v0.2.2 baseline preserved. No change to five-zone controls or opponent rules.
