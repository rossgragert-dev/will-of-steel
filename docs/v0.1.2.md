# v0.1.2 — Direct blade control

- The canvas is the full-screen sword surface. The left-thumb parry button remains separate.
- Slow drags reposition the blade; quick sweeps trigger speed-gated cuts. The offensive blade tip is projected onto the touch point. The defensive blade center tracks touch, while the blade aligns to the chosen high, low, side or center guard.
- Stationary center hold performs one lunge; movement and stance changes disarm the held gesture. Fast cuts use short windup and recovery. Entering defense cancels direct-cut recovery.
- The earlier mobile zoom hotfix stays in place.
- The combat outcome is still timing/guard-based, not physical blade collision. Visual fidelity, real iPhone touch feel and threshold tuning require device testing.
