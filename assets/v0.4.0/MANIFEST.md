# v0.4.0 Asset Manifest

The repository is **public**: every asset listed here is original work, generated by the scripts in `tools/blender/` for Will of Steel (textures are numpy-generated from fixed seeds). There are no third-party or purchased assets and no motion capture.

Conventions: metres. In Blender, Z is up and characters face -Y; exports are glTF Y-up with characters facing +Z. Exports are never overwritten (each version gets a new `_vNNN` file). Each `*.report.json` beside an export holds machine-generated checksums, counts and IK checks. Previews are in `previews/v0.4.0/`, and editable sources in `source/blender/`.

## Superseded iterations (kept, not for use)
- longsword v001–v005: v001–v003 had preview-lighting problems. v001–v005 all have all-zero roughness maps (a colour-space bug, fixed in v006).
- ser_aldren v001 (failed build, textures only) and v002–v005: normal-map sawtooth (v002–v003), zero roughness maps (v002–v004), and cloth poking through plates (v005).
- william_fp_arms v001: arms outside the portrait frame, and the enemy preview faced away. v002: low guards and head guard hid the opponent. v003: the occlusion scoring was invalid (a raster bug).
